Our Story

Named after the iconic phrase from The Great Gatsby, Old Sport Barbers opened its doors in 2014 on Victoria Street, Darlinghurst. Founded by Sasi, who brings decades of expertise from running one of Darlinghurst's top salons, we've built a reputation for quality craftsmanship and classic style.

Step into our shop and experience the art of traditional barbering combined with modern techniques. Whether you're after a classic cut, a sharp fade, or a relaxing steam facial, we've got you covered.
